Your tap, your beer

Whatever's on, wear it with pride.

Put any badge on the screen and Tapstr shows it full size, with the fridge temperature and pints left dropping in for a quick glance.

02

Health at a glance

Know it's pouring well before you pull.

Pints left, fridge and line temperature, gas pressure and days since the last clean. One health score tells you if all's well, and when it isn't, tap FIX to see what's wrong and how to put it right.

  1. All healthy
  2. Needs a tweak
  3. Not pouring
  4. Tap FIX
03

Line cleaning

Clean lines, clean pints.

Tapstr counts the days since your line was last cleaned and lets you know when it's due. Start a clean, flush the line and tap Done. Flow monitoring pauses, so cleaning water never counts as beer.

  1. Days since last clean
  2. Cleaning
  3. All clean
04

Tap a new keg

New keg? Three taps and you're pouring.

Pick its size, confirm, and you're off. Tapstr resets the count and tracks every pint from the first one.

  1. Pick the keg size
  2. Confirm
  3. Pouring
05

Temperature control

Dial in the perfect pour.

Tapstr replaces your fridge thermostat. Set upper and lower limits, or let SmartTemp learn your fridge's cooling cycle to hold the temperature steady and go easy on the compressor.

  1. Thermostat
  2. SmartTemp
  3. Set
06

More taps

Got more than one tap?

Switch the new one on and your Tapstr spots it straight away. Add it in a couple of taps and it joins the WiFi you already have, with no passwords to type.

  1. New tap found
  2. Added

Always up to date

Gets better on its own.

Tapstr keeps itself up to date. New features and fixes arrive over WiFi and install in a few moments.
